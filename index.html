Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Counter & Scan ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using our application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of info we collect include:

  • Media Assets: Images and photos you select for OCR text extraction. These are processed via encrypted channels and are never stored permanently on our servers.
  • Extracted Text: The content generated from your scans. This is processed in transient memory for analysis (word counting, keyword extraction) and returned to you.
  • Usage Data: We may collect diagnostic info and usage stats (e.g., app crashes, feature usage frequency) via standard mobile analytics.

2. Third-Party Services and AI

Our app integrates specialized third-party services:

  • Google Gemini AI (OCR): We use Gemini API for high-accuracy text extraction. For Free Tier users, data may be processed according to Google's standard terms. For Pro users, we utilize API configurations that prioritize data privacy and minimize data retention.
  • Google AdMob: We use AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers (IDFA/AAID) to serve personalized ads. You can manage these permissions in your system settings.
  • RevenueCat: Handles subscription management. They see your purchase status and a unique anonymous identifier, but never your direct payment info (Credit Card, etc.).

3. Data Security and Retention

We prioritize the security of your data. All communication with our servers is encrypted using Industry-standard SSL/TLS technology. We operate on a "Privacy by Design" principle, meaning we avoid collecting personal identifiable information (PII) like names or addresses unless you explicitly provide them (e.g., via support email).

4. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

Depending on your location, you have rights to access, delete, or limit the use of your data. Since we do not maintain user accounts or store scan history on our servers, your "data" mostly exists locally on your device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
