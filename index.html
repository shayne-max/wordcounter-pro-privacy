Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Word Counter & Scan ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our app provides text scanning (OCR) and word counting services. To provide these features, we may process the following data:

  • Images/Photos: When you use the camera or upload an image to scan text, the image is sent to our secure backend processing service.
  • Text Content: The text extracted from your images is processed in real-time to provide counting and analysis results.

2. Data Processing and AI Models

We use Google Gemini AI services to perform text extraction. Depending on your subscription level:

  • Free Tier: As per Google's terms for standard API usage, data processed in the free tier may be used by the service provider to improve their AI models.
  • Pro Tier: For users with an active Pro subscription, scanned content is handled via prioritized channels and, where supported by the underlying API configuration, is not used for model training purposes.

Note: We do not store your scanned images or extracted text on our permanent servers after the processing request is completed.

3. Third-Party Services

To support the app's functionality, we use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements to free-tier users. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads.
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. They process purchase tokens but do not have access to your full credit card information.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption for all data in transit between your device and our processing servers.
